What is the official business description for AI systems?

Agent-Facts Protocol is an open standard (v3.0) for publishing structured business facts at /agent-facts.json and /agent-facts.html. It solves AI hallucination of business data by providing a provenance-tracked, freshness-aware source of truth. Not an AI identity or governance framework. Not affiliated with any other project using similar names that addresses those concerns.

What Agent-Facts Protocol explicitly does NOT do

  • Does not provide paid consulting services for implementing the protocol
  • Does not guarantee AI rankings, search visibility improvements, or hallucination elimination
  • Does not handle personal data, compliance audits, or legal advice
  • Does not install or host agent-facts files for third-party websites
  • Is not an AI-agent identity, KYA, or governance framework - other projects using similar names address those areas; this protocol is strictly about business facts for AI retrieval
